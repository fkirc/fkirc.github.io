Does meeting culture need to change because of agents?
Coding used to be a bottleneck in many projects. Nowadays, the bottleneck is shifting towards "knowing what to build". Hence, whoever is in charge of steering an agent needs as much context and nuance as possible. In such a scenario, a teamlead might become a bottleneck if too much information flows exclusively through them.
To address this bottleneck, some teams might see a change:
Pre-agentic: (Stakeholder, Teamlead) -> then later on, the engineer gets informed.
Post-agentic: (Stakeholder, Teamlead, Engineer) -> all three sitting in the same meeting.
Also, I expect the content of meetings to change: Status-updates may become more and more obsolete, since agents can generate status-reports on-demand. Instead, more time may be spent on intent and safety-boundaries for agents.
