Doing the ethically right thing can also pay off in the short term.

Crypto safety has made several foundational research advances in recent years. Still, the quantum threat against blockchains remains latent, and defenses are being deployed at only a "modest" pace.
Will a quantum fallout actually happen? We don't know, but the mere possibility may already hurt large-scale adoption.
In institutional finance, large-scale systematic risks never come for free. Investors price them in as a "virtual interest rate": an annualized risk premium.
Deploying quantum defenses at scale could therefore bring immediate financial benefits and sustained growth, because that risk premium may drop right away.

The same holds in other industries. For example, a real estate developer who factors in accelerated global warming today may immediately get lower interest rates and insurance premiums on long-term loans.
So the financial benefit arrives today, not only for a future generation saving a house from flooding or a blockchain from collapse.
