Most of the times I post about tech-topics.
But only in one single post I declare my political stand:
There does exist "good" and "bad".
I reject a nihilist view of the world and I reject moral relativism where every judgement depends on the "point of view". I also reject the framing that "there are always two sides to a fight", since a conflict can have a clear aggressor and a clear victim.
Saying that everyone in political power is "equally bad" is a denial of reality that helps tyranny and conspiracy theories. A liberal constitutional state can preserve law and order and stay close to factual truth. In contrast, regimes like the current Russian dictatorship are built on a pile of lies. What we need against those regimes is not a "factchecking", since it is anyways barely possible to argue against conspiracy-believers. Neither can a naive appeasement stop authoritarian aggressions. Instead, only an ironclad stance for the rule of law can protect the world from backsliding into tyranny. This can coexist with strong ambition and with human nature. The fascist logic of domination by force might have been survivable for small pre-state tribes, but not in a world holding nuclear weapons and year by year stronger AI-models.
Also in economics, a socially fair re-distribution can only co-exist with certain liberties.
