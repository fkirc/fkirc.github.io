Testing and QA is being "fun" again (as fun as a professional task can realistically be)! For many years, I was kind of bored by the repetitive work of writing tests. But now, thanks to AI-agents, it starts becoming an algorithmic and statistical problem that I like to treat with my computer science background. New testing strategies can therefore be trialed with low effort and discarded in case they do not work. Perhaps we might see a new "golden age" of end-to-end tests that is still unexplored.
