Can we convert tokens into quality? Partially. Throwing tokens onto a problem will not magically achieve quality if the design is flawed. But in the case of regression-tests, agents can be extremely effective. In the old world, creating a "regression suite" might have required long alignment-meetings and weeks of implementations. Nowadays, a smart QA-engineer can trial new ideas within a single afternoon. In the meetings, the teams can then debate about already working test-prototypes, instead of debating for what type of tests the budget should be allocated. My takeaway is clear: Let us use agents to increase quality, instead of only debating how fast a feature can be churned out.
