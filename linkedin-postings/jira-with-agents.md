Enterprises are now using AI agents for almost everything — but Jira is not ready.

Ask an agent for your tasks, and Jira-MCP delivers:

- 58-second waiting
- custom fields like sprint not visible via MCP
- description overwritten by some other custom field

Traceability? Often gone. Years of customizations have left Jira as a walking dead in the era of agents.

Agents are making those customizations obsolete — systems that were already questionable for humans are now brutally exposed.

Agents thrive on clear conventions, not endless customization.

Jira taught us one thing: if your work system isn’t agent-friendly, it’s already obsolete.
